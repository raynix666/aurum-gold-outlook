Prices and currency

Aurum converts Narodowy Bank Polski’s daily fine-gold price from złoty to US dollars at the same day’s exchange rate. One troy ounce weighs 31.1034768 grams.

JOD prices use a fixed reference conversion of 0.709 dinar per US dollar, halfway between the Central Bank of Jordan’s published USD rates of 708 and 710 fils. Local dealer prices can include premiums and making charges.

Forecast model

Three ridge-regression models learn from 5-, 20- and 60-observation price changes and recent volatility. Each model uses up to 1,008 historical observations and blends its estimate equally with the latest reference price.

Prices and forecast ranges scale with gold content. One gram of 21K contains 0.875 grams of pure gold, and one gram of 18K contains 0.750 grams. Both follow the percentage changes in the 24K forecast.

Targets fall one week, one calendar month and three calendar months after the last quote. For historical targets on a market holiday, the model uses the last available quote on or before the target date.

Reading the range

Each endpoint range uses the 80th percentile of absolute historical log-price errors from rolling forecasts. It shows the scale of past misses. Future prices can move outside it.

The chart interpolates between horizon estimates in log-price space. The band between targets is illustrative. Unexpected events and changes in market conditions can affect accuracy.
